🔗 Connect Groww

ClaudeTrade needs your Groww Trade API key to see stock prices. It only reads prices while practising.

  1. Open groww.in/trade-api/api-keys and sign in.
  2. Click the little arrow next to Generate API Key → choose Generate TOTP token.
  3. Give it a name (like claudetrade) and continue.
  4. Copy the TOTP token into box 1 and the TOTP secret into box 2 below.

Keep these two secret, like a password. Never share them, not even in a chat.

    What is this?

    ClaudeTrade practises trading Indian stocks with pretend money (₹10,000) using real prices from Groww. Each trading day it watches a few big companies, makes small practice trades by itself, and closes all of them before 3:10 PM. Leave this computer on and awake on weekdays from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Real-money trading is OFF and can only be turned on in the Advanced view after enough safe practice.
